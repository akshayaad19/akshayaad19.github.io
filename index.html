About

I got the opportunity to join FourKites' AI team early, while the company was still figuring out what "AI-first" meant for supply-chain software. That meant sitting in architecture discussions for problems nobody had a playbook for yet, demoing early agent behavior directly to our CEO, and working hands-on across three different agent systems rather than owning one narrow lane.

A recurring part of the job: taking an ambiguous customer problem and turning it into an actual production requirement, then building the AI solution to match it — not just the version that technically runs. I'd rather get my hands dirty on something new and learn from it than wait for a fully scoped ticket.

I care about the part of AI engineering that doesn't demo well: why retrieval quietly breaks once an evidence set gets small, why a model's memory footprint blows past a hosting limit, why the same prompt returns a different answer twice. Finding those causes, in production, is the work I actually enjoy.
